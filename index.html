Scan a barcode. Build your library.

QuetzaLib scans the ISBN on the back of a book, looks up its title, author, and cover, and adds it to a personal library that lives entirely on your phone — no account, no cloud, no sync.

Everything happens on the shelf you already own

One scan gets a book into your library. From there QuetzaLib tracks what it looks like, where you are in it, and where you last saw it.

Scan-to-add

Point the camera at a barcode and the ISBN-10/13 is read and looked up automatically — or type/paste an ISBN if there's no barcode to scan.

Layered metadata lookup

Google Books first, Open Library as a fallback, and RanobeDB as a specialized last resort for light novels — resolved in the scanned ISBN's own language, not just English.

Cover & spine scanning

Capture a book's front, spine, and back into a reusable preset using an auto-detecting, auto-cropping document scanner — no more crooked cover photos.

Scan-to-fill OCR

Photograph the title, author, or publisher text and OCR fills the field — always shown back to you for approval before anything is written.

Preview before you save

A cover preset is auto-created from the looked-up thumbnail as soon as you scan a book, so you see what it'll look like on the shelf before committing.

Saved pages

Photograph a page or illustration worth remembering and keep it as an in-app reminder, with an optional label and note.

Reading-status stamps

A timeline of reading / finished / dropped / paused stamps per book, instead of one status field that overwrites your history.

Shelf view

Browse as a visual shelf of spines or covers, with a List/Shelf toggle and text fallback for books without a scanned cover yet.

Categories & sorting

Sort by title, author, date added, or series (down to the decimal volume), grouped by language and category, with live book counts in every filter.

Scan to find

Already own the book in your hand? Scan its barcode to jump straight to its shelf entry instead of typing a search.

Fully local

Every book, photo, and stamp lives in an on-device SQLite database. Nothing is synced anywhere unless you back it up yourself.

In-app updates

QuetzaLib isn't on the Play Store, so Settings → App update checks GitHub Releases and installs newer builds over the existing app.
